Sample 1 • Self-paced course

Interactive eLearning

Integrating Technology with the SAMR Model

Self-paced, ~10 minutes • objectives, chunked content, worked examples, a scored knowledge check, and a summary

A short, mobile-friendly module that teaches educators to classify a classroom technology use at the right SAMR level and to move a lesson up a level. Built end to end with adult-learning principles (clear objectives, examples, practice with immediate feedback).

Outcome: designed so that, after ten minutes, a learner can correctly classify a technology use in the SAMR model and propose one concrete change to deepen it. The built-in knowledge check gives each learner an immediate proficiency signal (score out of 4).

Sample 2 • Instructional video

Video • AI-assisted production

AI-produced video lesson

Scripted, designed, and produced end to end with an AI workflow (ChatGPT for scripting, NotebookLM for first-pass visuals, HeyGen for slide-to-video), with every pedagogical and quality decision kept under human judgment

A concise explainer-style video lesson created as a prototype to show how generative AI can speed production without ceding instructional quality.

Outcome: delivered a ready-to-review, under-five-minute video lesson prototype, and demonstrated an end-to-end AI production workflow that a small team can use to ship learning quickly while keeping accuracy and pedagogy under human control.
